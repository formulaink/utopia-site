Four ways members keep their advantage

Every seat includes a structured growth path, a monthly rhythm and a room worth showing up for.

Purpose

Path & Purpose online program

A self-paced program that gets clear on what you want, why it matters and where to focus next, so every quarter is pointed in the right direction.

Strategy

The Agile Accelerated Formula

The same agile framework Maria has taught to Fortune 100 teams, adapted for founders and investors who want to move faster and scale without burning out.

Monthly virtual mastermind calls

Connection

In-person mastermind evenings
