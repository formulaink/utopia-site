Utopia Penthouse

Las Vegas's most exclusive wellness members club and private event venue, on the entire 59th floor of Palms Place.

Overview

Utopia will turn the full-floor penthouse atop Palms Place into Las Vegas's most exclusive wellness members club and private event venue. The space is ready to open as-is, and investor funds buy it outright, so the business is built on a hard asset.

Utopia earns from three directions: a small, high-value members club, premium private buyouts during Las Vegas's biggest weeks, and a set of brand, wellness and media revenue streams.

The property

The entire 59th floor

The penthouse takes up the entire 59th floor of Palms Place, about 1.5 miles west of the Strip, with a private elevator, 360-degree views, more than 6,300 square feet inside, 4 king suites and a large wraparound terrace with a pool.

  • Proven demandThe penthouse already has a strong rental history with corporate and celebration events.
  • Ready to openFurnished and already listed for events, so Utopia launches with decor upgrades, not construction.
  • Location12 minutes from the Las Vegas Convention Center, home to CES and the city's largest trade shows.
